# Phase 0: Validate Before Coding

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks (Month 1, first half)

## Overview
- **Goal:** Prove that the product solves a real purchasing problem.
- **Focus:** This is NOT about proving AR is cool, but rather proving the business value.
- **Prerequisite:** Must complete before any engineering starts.

## Step 1: Retailer Research (Week 1-2)
- **Objective:** Identify and approach 20-30 hardware retailers.
- **Actions:**
  - [ ] Compile list of target retailers.
  - [ ] Schedule and conduct interviews.
  - [ ] Document findings in a structured format.

### Interview Script Template
Use the following questions to guide the conversation:
- How often do customers return hardware because it doesn't fit?
- What products cause the most confusion?
- How do salespeople currently recommend products?
- Do customers ask 'how will this look?'
- How often do customers need measurements?
- Would the retailer use an app during sales?
- Would they pay a monthly subscription?
- Would manufacturers provide 3D/CAD files?

## Step 2: Customer Research (Week 1-2)
- **Objective:** Interview 20-30 customers.
- **Actions:**
  - [ ] Create simple prototype/mockup (Figma wireframes).
  - [ ] Conduct customer interviews and surveys.
  - [ ] Document customer pain points.

### Customer Survey Template
- **Key Question:** "Would you use this before buying a ₹2,000-₹20,000 hardware product?"
- **Additional Questions:**
  - How do you currently visualize hardware before buying?
  - Have you ever bought hardware that didn't fit or look right?
  - What would make you use an AR app for this purpose?

## Step 3: Competitive Analysis (Week 2)
- **Objective:** Research existing AR furniture/hardware apps.
- **Actions:**
  - [ ] Analyze apps like IKEA Place, Houzz, etc.
  - [ ] Identify gaps in the door hardware specifically.
  - [ ] Document findings and competitive advantages.

## Step 4: Validation Metrics
Define clear success criteria for moving to Phase 1:
- **Retailer Interest:** Minimum 30% of retailers approached show strong interest or willingness to pilot.
- **Customer Utility:** Minimum 50% of customers indicate they would use the tool.
- **Pricing:** Validation that retailers would consider a monthly subscription model.

## Step 5: Early Partner Identification
- **Objective:** Secure initial partnerships for the pilot phase.
- **Actions:**
  - [ ] Identify 3-5 pilot retailers.
  - [ ] Obtain Letters of Intent (LOI) or verbal commitments.
  - [ ] Identify 1-2 hardware manufacturers willing to provide 3D assets/CAD files.

## Deliverables Checklist
- [ ] 20+ retailer interviews completed
- [ ] 20+ customer interviews completed
- [ ] Competitive analysis document completed
- [ ] Figma prototype/mockup created
- [ ] 3-5 pilot retailers identified
- [ ] Go/No-Go decision document finalized

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Can't find interested retailers** | High | Broaden outreach, refine value proposition focusing on reduced returns and increased sales. |
| **Customers don't see value** | High | Ensure prototype clearly demonstrates solving the "will it fit/look good" problem; iterate on feedback. |
| **Manufacturers won't share CAD files** | Medium | Offer to create initial 3D models in-house for pilot; emphasize marketing benefits to manufacturers. |

## Documentation & Analysis Framework
- **Retailer Insights:** Maintain a spreadsheet tracking contact info, interview dates, key quotes, and interest levels (Low/Medium/High).
- **Customer Insights:** Summarize survey data and highlight recurring themes and pain points.
- **Decision Matrix:** Compile all data into a Go/No-Go document that scores the validation metrics against the defined success criteria.
